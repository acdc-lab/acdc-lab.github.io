The 26th IEEE International Conference on Data Mining (ICDM 2026) took place in Shenyang, China, from November 12 to 15, 2026. Jing Fan of the Advanced Cross-media Data Computing Group (ACDC Lab) at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications attended the conference and gave a talk in the main program. [Conference program](https://icdm2026.neu.edu.cn/Program/)

ICDM is an international conference covering data mining theory, methods, and applications. The China Computer Federation (CCF) lists it as a Category B conference in databases, data mining, and information retrieval. This year's program included keynotes, paper presentations, workshops, and poster sessions. [CCF conference list](https://www.ccf.org.cn/Academic_Evaluation/DM_CS/) · [Program overview](https://icdm2026.neu.edu.cn/ProgramOverview/list.htm)

{% include news-photo.html src="/assets/img/activities/2026-11-icdm/photo2.jpg" caption="Jing Fan at ICDM 2026" %}

ACDC Lab's paper *Structure Attribute Seesaw: Conflict Aware Embedding Decoupling in Attributed Graph Clustering* was accepted as a long paper in the research track. The study addresses a challenge in attributed graph clustering: graph connections and node attributes sometimes suggest different ways to group the same nodes. The paper decouples the representations of these two sources of information to address that conflict.

On November 15, first author Jing Fan gave an oral presentation introducing the research problem and the proposed method. [Presentation schedule](https://icdm2026.neu.edu.cn/DetailedSessions/list.htm)
