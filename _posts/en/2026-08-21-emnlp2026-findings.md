Progress on multimodal entity alignment: **Bridging Reasoning and Retrieval: A Large-Small Model Collaboration Framework for Multimodal Entity Alignment** has been accepted as a Findings paper at EMNLP 2026.

This year's competition was fierce: EMNLP received 17,669 submissions, with 2,719 accepted to the Main Conference (15.4%) and 2,533 to Findings (14.3%).
