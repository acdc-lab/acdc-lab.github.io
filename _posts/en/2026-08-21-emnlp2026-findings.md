We are pleased to announce that our paper **Bridging Reasoning and Retrieval: A Large-Small Model Collaboration Framework for Multimodal Entity Alignment** has been accepted as a **Findings** paper at **EMNLP 2026**!

This year EMNLP received an unprecedented 17,669 submissions: 2,719 were accepted to the Main Conference (15.4%) and 2,533 to Findings (14.3%).
